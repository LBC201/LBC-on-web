“Good badminton is built on clear communication, sharp footwork, and a welcome for every player who walks through the door.”

Operating in Loughrea, Co. Galway, we run regular social and competitive sessions. Whether you’re picking up a racket for the first time or looking to improve your doubles game, there’s a place for you on court.

Get competitive games all year from friendly rallies to league and tournament preparation. New members are always welcome.
